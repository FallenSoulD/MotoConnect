Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the MotoConnect mobile application (the "App") and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

1. Information We Collect

While using Our App, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address
  • First name and last name
  • Location data (While using the app, to show nearby riders)
  • Usage Data

2. Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our App, including to monitor the usage of our App.
  • To manage Your Account: to manage Your registration as a user of the App.
  • To show nearby riders: We use your location data to provide core social features. Your location is only shared while the app is active or when you explicitly enable background tracking.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These include:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: For authentication and database management.
  • Google AdMob: To serve advertisements. AdMob may use advertising IDs to serve personalized ads.
  • RevenueCat: To securely process in-app purchases and subscriptions.

4. Deleting Your Data

You have the right to delete or request that We assist in deleting the Personal Data that We have collected about You. You can do this directly within the App's settings menu or by contacting us.

5. Child Safety & CSAM Policy

MotoConnect strictly prohibits the uploading, sharing, or distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or any content that exploits or endangers children. Our app is intended strictly for users aged 18 and older. Any account found to be associated with CSAM will be immediately terminated, and the incident, along with the user's data, will be reported to the relevant legal authorities (such as NCMEC or local law enforcement) without exception.

6. SOS & Roadside Assistance Features

MotoConnect includes a "Roadside Assistance" (SOS) feature designed solely to alert nearby community members when you need non-emergency help (e.g., flat tire, out of gas). This feature is NOT a substitute for official emergency services. In case of a real emergency, accident, or injury, you must contact local emergency authorities (e.g., 112, 911) directly. MotoConnect is not responsible or liable for any delays, failures, or damages arising from the use of this community-based assistance feature.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify You of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
